## What is not quoted

JLCPCB explicitly prevents proceeding to PCB Assembly until Gerber files are uploaded. PCBWay states components are excluded and final pricing follows file review. PCBWay's advanced-options section explicitly excludes functional testing, firmware loading and cable-harness work from the online quotation. Battery sourcing/preparation and lithium shipping are also unconfirmed. Thus the displayed subtotals cannot be treated as delivered, programmed fidget prices.

The existing Rev0 package is a design draft: it does not yet contain a routed native PCB, fabrication Gerbers, a finalized BOM, placement/CPL file, or flashable MCU firmware. These are work still owed by the designer, not files the customer is expected to supply. To obtain comparable full assembly quotes through both websites, finish and verify those deliverables, upload the same release package, request programming/testing/battery work, then compare vendor-reviewed totals before ordering.
