# Q4 enclosure and home completion

The enclosure is a **50 × 60 mm** body with a **19.6 mm display-cover height** and a provisional **30.4 mm keycap envelope height**. It fits the separate 42 × 54 × 1 mm Q4 PCB. The STEP assembly and PNGs use the actual board positions and simplified component envelopes; they are not exact vendor assembly CAD.

## Parts and hardware

| Item | Quantity | Specification / status |
|---|---:|---|
| Printed base, front display cover, rear key plate, battery keeper | 1 each | Four supplied STL files; print orientation is already applied |
| Board/cover screws | 4 | M2 × 12, nominal 4 mm head diameter / 1.7 mm height envelope |
| Battery-keeper screws | 2 | M2 × 5, same head envelope |
| Rear display supports | 2 | Compliant 2.5 mm-high annuli, 3.8 mm outside / 2.5 mm inside diameter; sample-fit material and component-free contact lands before use |
| OLED module | 1 | HS96L01W4S03 / C5139758, separately sourced loose |
| Header | 1 | PZ254V-11-07P / C492406; 2.5 mm body separates host and module PCBs |
| Key switches | 2 | CPG151101D13 / C49234235, separately sourced loose |
| Keycaps | 2 | Provisional MX-compatible 18.2 mm square envelopes; exact sourcing and travel fit unqualified |
| Prepared cell and NTC harness | 1 | Unqualified four-wire pack, maximum modeled 21 mm diameter × 4.2 mm height; see power/pack release gates |

The printed screw pilots are 1.7 mm. Thread formation, fastener head fit and repeated service strength must be established on a print sample; the model does not establish a reliable printed thread. Supports, screws and the prepared pack are separate from the PCB assembly BOM. Do not infer that JLC includes them or that public stock has been allocated.

## Assembly sequence

1. Print and inspect the four parts. Fit the keeper and screws around an inert pack-sized gauge first. Confirm screw engagement without protruding into the cell space. Qualify the actual pack and sensor before connecting it.
2. Seat both key switches in the **rear key plate before soldering** their four electrical leads to the host PCB. The plate is intentionally captured by the fitted switches. Check full switch travel and plate clips on the print sample.
3. With the front cover removed, solder the seven-pin header between the host PCB and display module. This is fourteen solder joints. Keep the 2.5 mm header body between the PCBs; trim exposed pin ends to **at most 1 mm** on both sides. Check all joints and orientation before power.
4. Fit the two compliant supports under the module's rear mounting lands after confirming those lands are free of components. Do not press on the glass or force the module into a warped position. The manufacturer's drawing, not its contradictory summary dimensions, controls the CAD.
5. Lower the PCB/key-plate assembly vertically into the base, route the qualified harness through the reserved side corridor, and secure the rear mounts. Leave the front cover off for inspection and USB programming. Use the **USB-first commissioning sequence** in the power document; cold battery insertion is not a validated workflow.
6. Lower the front display cover vertically and fit its two screws. Fit the keycaps and check full travel, USB cable access, the bottom reset pinhole and display visibility.

Home soldering totals **18 joints per device**. The two SMT-side USB shell joints are part of factory assembly; their stake projection and wetting still need first-article inspection.

## What the geometry checks establish

`fit-report.json` and `build-manifest.json` bind the actual native PCB/model, generator and firmware pixel renderer to the outputs. Checks require four valid single solids, manifold STL edges, bed-aligned print orientation, zero modeled static intersections and zero intersections in sampled straight vertical insertion/removal paths. The displayed sample count is generated by the actual production OLED pixel routine.

These checks do not establish print tolerances, screw/thread strength, display support material, solder quality, cable strain, battery retention, drops, keycap fit or wiring flexibility. The actual mating battery plug and flexible harness are represented by reserved corridors; their full insertion/bend behavior is not simulated. Component heights are conservative family envelopes. Inspect a physical first article before approving a production order.

Build with CadQuery 2.8, NumPy, Pillow and the KiCad Python bindings through `scripts/build_q4_enclosure.py`. A failed build removes its success manifest. Snapshot arguments are for private experiments; final review artifacts must use the repository's actual final routed board/model.
