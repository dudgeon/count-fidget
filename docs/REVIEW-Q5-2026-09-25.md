# Q5 engineering review — 25 September 2026

**Start here for Q5.** Q5 is a coordinated revision of the frozen Q4 candidate. It resolves the ten findings filed from the Q4 simulation review ([issues #2–#9, #12, #13](https://github.com/dudgeon/count-fidget/issues?q=is%3Aissue)) and the owner's requests of 25 September:
- soft power on the COUNT key with automatic power-off;
- a battery gauge, low-battery warning and storage cut-off;
- charge status;
- a reachable hardware reset;
- new key logic (increment wins; reset needs a deliberate hold);
- a shrunk enclosure;
- a BOM of JLC inventory parts only.

Q1–Q4 files are unchanged.

The design is **locked** as of the evening of 25 September (see [Design lock](#design-lock--25-september-evening)). JLCPCB quoting was then authorized for two variants only:
- variant A: JLC does all SMT and the user solders the display;
- variant B: JLC assembles everything.

The result is in [JLC-QUOTE-Q5](../procurement/q5/JLC-QUOTE-Q5.md). No order, payment, allocation, manufacturing release or other-vendor contact is authorized. Everything here is design-file, host-model, emulator and CAD evidence. **Nothing has been built or measured.**

**26 September:** the owner ordered a first-article batch of 5 assembled boards from JLCPCB ($298.08 including shipping; see [JLC-QUOTE-Q5](../procurement/q5/JLC-QUOTE-Q5.md)). The [first-article test plan](q5-first-article-test-plan.md) applies when the boards arrive.

Related documents: [Q5 power design](q5-power-design.md), [Q5 firmware and home programming](q5-firmware.md), [enclosure and assembly](../mechanical/q5/README.md), [build workflow](build-and-verify.md#active-q5-engineering-workflow), [Q4 simulation review that raised the issues](REVIEW-Q4-simulation-2026-09-25.md).

## What Q5 is

- **Unchanged from Q4:**
  - STM32L072CBT6 with ROM USB DFU;
  - FM25V02A SPI FRAM with the same 96-slot journal (a Q4 FRAM image reads unchanged);
  - HS96L01W4S03 SPI OLED module;
  - BQ25185 charger, BQ29700 protector, TLV767 3.2 V rail;
  - two-layer 42 × 54 mm board and the same four mounting holes, now **1.6 mm** thick (required by the hot-swap sockets).
- **Parts count:** **95 references: 81 fitted (80 vendor SMT + DS1) and 14 PCB features**, 41 exact MPNs.
  - The key switches are no longer soldered. SW1/SW2 are vendor-placed MX hot-swap sockets, and the loose clicky switches K1/K2 press into them.
- **Home completion:**
  - variant A: 14 joints (the display header);
  - variant B: none (JLC fits DS1).
- **Offboard items:** reduced to the user-supplied LIR2032 cell.

## Fix order and why

Each fix was placed so that nothing done earlier had to be redone:

1. **Architecture/netlist decisions first** (#12, #13, #8, #5): power latch, cell holder + onboard NTC, battery sense, charger status, QOD. These change pins, so firmware and layout depend on them.
2. **Firmware** against the new pin contract:
   - boot capture gate (#2);
   - key semantics and DFU safety (#3 and the owner's reset rule);
   - option-byte provisioning (#6);
   - responsiveness (#7);
   - power-off, gauge and cut-off (#12, #9.4);
   - PVD wording (#9.1).
3. **Instruction-level emulation** of the actual ELF on a Q5 board model, before any layout work, so behavioural bugs were found cheaply. Four firmware defects were found and fixed here: the mV overflow, the label priority, the USB-during-LO cancel, and release-loop flapping.
4. **PCB placement, routing, ERC/DRC** once the netlist was final.
5. **Enclosure** from the final routed board (#4, pack-bay removal, keycaps, reset pinhole, shrink).
6. **Stock screen and exports** bound to the final model hash.
7. **Documents and issues.**

## First-order de-risking — 26 September

Before the first paid order, the owner asked for more certainty. Three independent checks ran on the locked design:

| Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Datasheet pinout audit** of every active and polarised part (MCU pins and SPI alternate functions, charger, LDO, protector, comparator, switches, FRAM, ESD, diodes, FETs, USB-C, holder, sockets, display module) | **No pin-assignment errors.** The standard footprints are pad-for-pad identical to the official KiCad library, and the custom ones match the vendor drawings. | [pinout audit](first-order-derisk/pinout-audit-2026-09-26.md) |
| **Footprints against JLC's own EasyEDA footprints** (all 43 LCSC parts) | **No pad-order or mirror errors.** The raw KiCad CPL, however, would show ~15 polarised parts with the wrong pin 1 in JLC's preview, and SW1/SW2, J1 and DS1 offset. JLC-corrected CPLs were generated, and a preview checklist is in [JLC-QUOTE-Q5](../procurement/q5/JLC-QUOTE-Q5.md). | [footprint check](../scripts/q5_jlc_footprint_check/REPORT.md) |
| **Dead-on-arrival review** of the power-up paths, back-feeding, programming and latch failure modes | **No defect likely to leave a board dead or unrecoverable.** USB-only power-up, DFU entry (crystal-less, BOOT0 only) and the SWD fallback pads are confirmed. | this section |
| **Latch transient** (behavioural ngspice, bouncing contacts, 54 corners) | 120/120 bouncing presses latched. The worst-case minimum press is 14.5 ms with 10 nF (20.0 ms with 4.7 nF). USB alone turns the board on at every corner. | [q5-latch-spice](../simulation/q5-latch-spice/README.md) |

The findings led to these changes, all re-verified through the full chain: model, ERC, re-route, DRC/parity, verifier corruptions, emulator, stock, exports and enclosure.
- **R39 1k + C9:** filters U5's supply. U5 is a TLV7012 with a 6 V absolute maximum, and it was on raw VBUS, exposed to USB hot-plug ringing.
- **C40 4.7 → 10 nF:** doubles the bounce bridge and cuts the minimum latch press to 10.5 ms typical.
- **JLC-corrected placement files** plus a preview checklist.
- **Flasher and skill:** a clicker left in DFU mode must be reset with the pinhole *before* unplugging. The ROM's D+ pull-up back-feeds VBUS through the USBLC6 and holds the latch on, draining the cell.
- **Doc corrections:** the SSD1315 VBAT absolute maximum is 6 V (4.5 V is the recommended maximum), and the BQ25185 battery UVLO (~3.0 V) comes before the protector's 2.8 V.

Accepted and documented rather than changed:
- **Display module at 3.15–3.26 V.** The module's external input is specified at 3–5 V. The 3.5–4.2 V figure is the controller's internal pump input, and the module's internal power tree is undocumented. First-article test E1.
- **STAT pull-ups.** The MCU's internal ~40 kΩ is outside TI's 1–20 kΩ recommendation, but it is adequate for a static status read.
- **VDDA.** It has 100 nF only; ST also recommends 1 µF. The 10 µF bulk capacitor sits on the same rail.
- **No watchdog.** Firmware can be updated after assembly.

Recovery routes if something is wrong on the first boards:
- **SWD:** TP3 SWDIO, TP4 SWCLK, TP11 NRST and TP1/TP2 accept a standard ST-Link.
- **Latch control fails:** bridging TP13 (SYS_ON) to TP6 (SYS) holds the board on.
- **Stuck in DFU:** the pinhole resets it.

## Key and display behaviour (user-visible summary)

- **COUNT** (+ keycap) adds one; from off it powers on and that press counts. Held keys never repeat.
- **RESET** (○ keycap): hold 2 s until **RST** appears, then release (within 10 s) to zero. Pressing COUNT at any time cancels it.
- **Top-right:** 4-bar battery gauge. **LO** below ~3.55 V; below 3.35 V the count is saved and the board turns off. **CHG** while charging.
- **Auto-off:** 30 s after the last key on battery. With USB, the display sleeps and the MCU stops.
- **Label priority:** OPT > RST > ERR > MAX > LO > CHG/BAT.

## Physical gates before an order can be released

The Q4 gates still apply: USB/target execution, supply/interface behaviour, charger/protector/temperature, count and display reliability, assembly and enclosure. Q5 adds or changes these:

1. **Power latch:** minimum press to latch with a real bouncing MX contact; clean U7 turn-off on PB2 release; USB unplug during Stop; the NRST power cycle on battery.
2. **Battery sense and status:** ADC accuracy through the 75 kΩ divider source; gauge thresholds against a real LIR2032 discharge under display load; STAT1/STAT2 behaviour in every charger state; measured ~8 µA off current.
3. **Cell and holder:** holder contact resistance and retention; insertion with USB present (BQ2970 first connection); the retained cold-insertion counterexample on real cells; TH1 thermal coupling to the cell.
4. **Display supply (#8):** module power tree; VBAT, pump, readability and current at 3.15–3.26 V; 200 × NRST with the display on.
5. **Enclosure:** real USB-C cables in both orientations; printed keycap cross-socket fit and travel; pinhole access to SW3; load supports; cell replacement.
6. **Process:** BT1, J1 and hot-swap socket solder inspection; variant B acceptance of the stacked THT display. The PCB price was observed on JLC's quote page, and PCBA is estimated from published fees; the parts-matched PCBA quote needs the owner's signed-in upload ([JLC-QUOTE-Q5](../procurement/q5/JLC-QUOTE-Q5.md)).

The full first-article procedure is [q5-first-article-test-plan.md](q5-first-article-test-plan.md).
