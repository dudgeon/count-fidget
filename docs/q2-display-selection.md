## Necessary DE188 work and remaining risk

1. **Bias hardware:** add the three nominal **100 nF** R13/R23/R33-to-ground reservoirs at MCU pins **7/8/9**. Retain the existing nominal 100 nF pump capacitor unless further review changes it. [TI datasheet §8.12.8.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/msp430fr4133.pdf) and [family guide Figure 17-9](https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/slau445i/slau445i.pdf) support this correction.
2. **Timing:** use the separately verified **32 Hz** candidate. Its calculated REFO-tolerance range **30.88–33.12 Hz** fits the MCU and exact display recommendations. This does not measure the physical waveform or optical response.
3. **Footprint:** the [DE188 Rev4 drawing, p3](https://display-elektronik.de/filter/DE188-RU-30_75_3V.pdf) implies nominal **13.0 mm** lead-row centers, versus Q1's 13.5 mm. Confirm the interpretation and lead/hole tolerance stack; do not blindly shrink the existing drills. See [LCD audit](review-lcd-firmware-Q2.md).
4. **Response:** Rev4 p4 lists **440 ms total turn-on plus turn-off response**. That remains a meaningful fast-click visibility concern; changing frame frequency does not establish a faster glass response. The Lumex drawing does not supply a qualified optical-response comparison. Verify actual rapid counting, contrast, DC balance, temperature and current on a first article before accepting the display experience.

[NXP AN12579 §4](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN12579.pdf) demonstrates the Lumex display in an active low-power development-board configuration at 7 µA. This supports a passive-LCD direction; it is not a bare-display specification, Count Fidget current measurement or runtime promise.
