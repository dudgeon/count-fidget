### Concrete unresolved electrical questions

- **I²C:** the selected4.7k pull-ups require more sink current than the display's100µA output-low test establishes. A hypothetical6kΩ sink meets that table at100µA yet gives about1.68V acknowledgement with4.7k at3V, outside guaranteed MCU low recognition. It is a specification-consistent counterexample, not a measured OLED resistance. Weaker pull-ups alone do not establish compliance with the300ns rise limit and unknown bus capacitance.
- **Battery insertion:** a4.2V,20µF effective SYS,3.3Ω sense plus1Ω other-series-resistance model exceeds0.4V at the protector sense for about179.45µs, longer than its125µs minimum short-circuit delay. Actual cell impedance, capacitance, charger/protector gate slew and recovery are not established. TPS63900's10mA average input setting does not limit charging its input capacitor.
- **OLED operating budget:** the converter limit has no guaranteed min/max setting bound and sparse pixel current is unmeasured. Rail droop, pump startup and readability require proof under the intended load; a nominal efficiency calculation is not qualification.

Retain the cell/protection threshold,18.2mA charge stability, installed temperature sensing/fault response, capacitor effective values, sudden VDD collapse/FRAM, battery pack, actual assembly and runtime/mass gates. The firmware cannot preserve a newly detected volatile input-history gap if power disappears before its first marker write. The detailed reviews identify practical test and redesign branches; do not silently close those issues.

## Cost and USB programming decision

Two layers are already implemented. Retain one shared Spy-Bi-Wire programmer as the simplest current home-flashing path. Home soldering of the two switches is permissible if the actual savings justify it, but the observed manual-labor lines total only$5.06 per five-unit batch and include other work; the full$16.42 fixture is not necessarily avoidable. No savings quote is implied.

USB-C currently charges only. MSP430FR4133 has no native USB. Adding a USB-to-UART bridge would require bootloader-entry wiring and resolution of P1.0/P1.1 sharing with the buttons; a wrong bootloader password can erase information FRAM and the saved count. A native-USB MCU is a larger board/firmware/power redesign. The user's question does not select either change. Detailed constraints and primary references are in the firmware and home-completion notes.
